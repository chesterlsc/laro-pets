## Patterns
- Camera: floor-level / low 3/4 (feather pop + crouch in frame); top-down for multi-cat chaos. Owner handheld POV outperforms tripod seller demos on engagement.
- The beat: wait (frozen, butt-wiggle) → feather emerges → pounce; showing the full 5–8 s wait wins (Felix: 138 likes/1.2K views vs seller 114/60.7K).
- Sound: built-in squeak/chirp is hook ("come running when they hear the squeak" https://www.chewy.com/pawspik-interactive-hide-seek/dp/1669334); owner clips keep raw audio.
- Cats: Bengal, orange tabby, tuxedo, kittens, multi-cat; "cat who never plays" = strongest proof.
- Length: TikTok 8–20 s; YouTube 3–8 min.
- Hooks: (1) "My cat is OBSESSED with this…"; (2) cat-POV "Something was hiding under there and I had to INVESTIGATE" https://www.tiktok.com/@purrfellowcat/video/7662545951030086925 ; (3) "Held her attention for an hour — I finally had to turn it off" / "my cats all came over the second this turned on"; skeptic-convert "I wasn't sure my cat would even care… now it's part of her routine".

## Objections to pre-empt
- Chirp can't be silenced; mat slides/bunches so feather stalls; one cat terrified; burnt string end; replacement feathers out of stock (Chewy reviews).
- Wand barely moves; breaks/won't charge within a month; battery varies (consumerability).
- Feather safety / dye concerns (15K and 2.5M-view clips) → non-toxic dye + supervised play.
- "Cat ignored it" → catnip, slow intro, day-1 vs day-3 clip; 30-day "cat didn't play" refund; spare feathers in box.
