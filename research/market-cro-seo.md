## CRO evidence
a. Video: 85% convinced to buy by video; 96% watched explainer (Wyzowl 2026 n=266) https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/ ; 73% more likely after how-it-works video (Invesp, secondary) https://www.invespcro.com/blog/e-commerce-product-videos/ ; Baymard: many users won't watch video → supplement images https://baymard.com/blog/in-scale-product-images
b. Sticky ATC mobile: +4.09% CR (The Good May 2025) https://thegood.com/insights/sticky-add-to-cart/ ; +2.74% (Traction Oct 2025) https://tractionmarketing.nz/insights/unlocking-small-wins-that-scale-what-we-learned-from-testing-a-sticky-add-to-cart-button-on-mobile/
c. Free shipping: top abandonment reason "extra costs too high" 40%; "delivery too slow" 20%; avg abandonment 70.22% (Baymard) https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate ; 73.9% pick free shipping as most important; 71.2% of Top-1000 offer it (DC360/Bizrate Oct 2024) https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/23/who-offers-free-shipping-in-ecommerce/ ; 93% take action to qualify (Capital One Shopping, secondary) https://capitaloneshopping.com/research/free-shipping-statistics/
d. Bundles/recs: 7% of visits click recs → 26% revenue, +10% AOV (Salesforce) http://answers.salesforce.com/blog/2017/11/personalized-product-recommendations-drive-just-7-visits-26-revenue.html ; recs ≈31% of revenue (Barilliance via Shopify) https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-product-recommendation ; post-purchase upsell AOV +25% https://www.shopify.com/blog/post-purchase-upsell
e. Trust: 19% abandon over card trust (Baymard 2025 n=1,026); encapsulated payment section raises perceived security https://baymard.com/blog/perceived-security-of-payment-form ; PayPal seal 25%, Google Trusted Store 15% (CXL n=2,100) https://cxl.com/research-study/trust-seals/ ; 30-day guarantee +26% (Conversion Fanatics, anecdotal) https://conversionfanatics.com/does-a-money-back-guarantee-matter/ ; insufficient payment methods 9% of abandonments (Baymard); Shop Pay presence +5% https://www.shopify.com/blog/shop-pay-checkout
f. Reviews: 5 reviews → +270% purchase likelihood; sweet spot 4.0–4.7★; verified badge +15% (Spiegel) https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/how-online-reviews-influence-sales/ ; 91% read reviews; 45% won't buy without; 77% trust customer photos/videos (PowerReviews 2023 n=8,153) https://www.powerreviews.com/power-of-reviews-2023/
g. Speed: 0.1 s mobile gain → +8.4% retail CR, +9.2% AOV (Deloitte/Google 2020) https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/9757/Milliseconds_Make_Millions_report_hQYAbZJ.pdf ; 1 s = 3.05% CR vs 1.12% at 3 s (Portent 2022) https://portent.com/blog/analytics/research-site-speed-hurting-everyones-revenue.htm
h. Urgency: honest delivery-cutoff line +27.1% revenue (Speero, dog-food store) https://speero.com/post/implementing-urgency-on-ecommerce-product-pages-for-a-27-1-lift-case-study ; "act now" dark patterns no lift + backlash (Luguri & Strahilevitz 2021) https://www.deceptive.design/articles/shining-a-light-on-dark-patterns ; discount alone 56.0% vs 45.6% with countdown (thesis, secondary) https://cleancommit.io/blog/do-countdown-timers-work/
i. Above fold: 57% viewing time above fold, 74% first two screens (NN/g 2018) https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/ ; 52% of sites lack descriptive text on images; 56% go to images first https://baymard.com/blog/product-images-descriptive-text ; 28% lack in-scale image, 42% try to judge size https://baymard.com/blog/in-scale-product-images ; 62% of mobile product pages mediocre or worse (Baymard Mar 2026) https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux
j. Checkout: 18% abandon over forced account, 17% long checkout; better checkout +35.26% CR (Baymard); avg 5.1 steps, 11.3 fields vs ~8 needed https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields ; 42% interrupt with account prompts https://baymard.com/blog/delayed-account-creation

Benchmarks: overall 3.15%, desktop 3.62%, mobile 2.94%, Pet Care & Vet 3.0% (Dynamic Yield) https://marketing.dynamicyield.com/benchmarks/conversion-rate/ ; Smart Insights Jan 2025: 2.9% overall; cart abandonment 79% mobile vs 68.1% desktop; 75% sessions mobile https://www.smartinsights.com/ecommerce/ecommerce-analytics/ecommerce-conversion-rates/
